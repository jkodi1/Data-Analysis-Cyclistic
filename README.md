# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis Case Study

## 🚲 Project Overview
This repository contains my solution to the **Google Analytic Case Study 1**, based on the **Cyclistic** bike-share dataset. The goal of this project was to understand how casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes differently, in order to inform new marketing strategies aimed at converting casual riders into annual members.

## 📋 Google's 6 Phase Framework
### 1. Ask
* **Business Task:** Analyse 12 months of historical trip data to identify trends in how casual riders and annual members use bikes differently.
* **Stakeholders:** Cyclistic executive team and the Director of Marketing.

### 2. Prepare
* Data sourced from Motivate International Inc.
* The dataset covers 12 months of trip records including start/end times, station names, and rider types.

### 3. Process
* Cleaned data for inconsistencies (missing values, duplicate entries).

### 4. Analyze & 5. Share
* **Key Finding 1:** Casual riders tend to take longer trips, especially on weekends.
* **Key Finding 2:** Annual members use the service consistently for commuting during weekdays.
* **Key Finding 3:** Seasonal trends show a significant spike in casual usage during the summer months.

### 6. Act
* Recommended targeted digital marketing during weekends.
* Suggested seasonal memberships to capture the high-volume casual summer riders.
